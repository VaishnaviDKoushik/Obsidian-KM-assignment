The HR department plays an important role in improving employee capabilities.

1. Identifies training needs.
2. Prepares training plans and budgets.
3. Selects trainers and training methods.
4. Organizes training programmes.
5. Maintains training records.
6. Evaluates training effectiveness.
7. Supports performance appraisal.
8. Helps employees plan their careers.
9. Identifies future leaders.
10. Ensures training supports organizational goals.

Training evaluation is the process of measuring whether a training programme has achieved its objectives and improved employee performance.

### [[Objectives of Training Evaluation]]

1. Measure learning outcomes.
2. Identify improvements in job performance.
3. Determine whether training objectives were achieved.
4. Identify weaknesses in the training programme.
5. Assess the value of training expenditure.
6. Improve future training programmes.

### [[Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Training Evaluation]]

**1. Reaction**

Measures how participants felt about the training.

Example: Asking employees whether the training was useful.

**2. Learning**

Measures the knowledge or skills gained through training.

Example: Conducting a test after a computer course.

**3. Behaviour**

Measures whether employees apply what they learned at work.

Example: Observing whether employees use the new software correctly.

**4. Results**

Measures the effect of training on organizational outcomes.

Example: Checking whether reporting errors decreased after training.

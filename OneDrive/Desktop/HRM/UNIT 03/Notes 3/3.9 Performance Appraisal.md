### Meaning

Performance appraisal is the systematic process of evaluating an employee's job performance against established standards or objectives over a particular period.

It helps organizations understand employees' strengths, weaknesses, achievements, and development needs.

### [[Objectives of Performance Appraisal]]

1. Evaluate employee performance.
2. Provide feedback.
3. Identify training needs.
4. Support promotion and career decisions.
5. Recognize good performance.
6. Identify areas requiring improvement.
7. Improve communication between employees and supervisors.
8. Support decisions about rewards and development.

### [[Methods of Performance Appraisal]]

**1. Rating Scale Method**

Employees are rated against specific performance factors, such as quality, punctuality, and teamwork.

**2. Ranking Method**

Employees are ranked according to their relative performance.

**3. Checklist Method**

The evaluator uses a list of statements to assess employee behaviour or performance.

**4. Critical Incident Method**

The evaluator records important examples of effective or ineffective employee behaviour.

**5. Management by Objectives (MBO)**

Managers and employees agree on measurable objectives and review the results achieved.

**6. 360-Degree Feedback**

Feedback is collected from several sources, such as supervisors, colleagues, subordinates, and sometimes customers.

**7. Self-Appraisal**

Employees assess their own performance and identify their achievements and development needs.

### [[Performance Appraisal Process]]

1. Set performance standards and objectives.
2. Communicate expectations to employees.
3. Measure actual performance.
4. Compare results with established standards.
5. Discuss performance with the employee.
6. Identify strengths and improvement areas.
7. Prepare a development plan.
8. Review progress.

### Advantages of Performance Appraisal

- Clarifies performance expectations.
- Recognizes employee achievements.
- Identifies training needs.
- Supports career development.
- Improves communication.
- Helps managers make informed decisions.

### Limitations of Performance Appraisal

- Personal bias may affect ratings.
- Standards may be unclear.
- Recent events may influence the evaluation excessively.
- Employees may feel stressed about the process.
- Poorly conducted appraisals may reduce motivation.

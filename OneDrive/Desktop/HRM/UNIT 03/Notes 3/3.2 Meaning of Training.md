Training is a systematic process of improving employees' knowledge, skills, and abilities required to perform their current jobs effectively.

### [[Objectives of Training]]

1. To improve employee performance.
2. To develop job-related skills.
3. To increase productivity.
4. To reduce mistakes and accidents.
5. To introduce employees to new technology.
6. To improve the quality of work.
7. To increase employee confidence.
8. To prepare employees for changing job requirements.

### [[Importance of Training]]

1. **Improves Efficiency:** Employees learn to perform tasks more effectively.
2. **Reduces Errors:** Proper training reduces mistakes and wastage.
3. **Increases Productivity:** Employees can complete work efficiently.
4. **Improves Quality:** Training helps maintain work standards.
5. **Builds Confidence:** Employees become more confident in their duties.
6. **Supports Technology:** Employees learn to use new tools and software.
7. **Improves Safety:** Employees learn correct safety procedures.
8. **Increases Job Satisfaction:** Employees feel more capable of performing their work.

### Example

A company provides software training to its employees so they can use a new computer application correctly.

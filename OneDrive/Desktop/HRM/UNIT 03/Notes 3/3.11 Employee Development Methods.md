Employee development methods help workers prepare for greater responsibilities and long-term career growth.

### [[Main Methods]]

1. **Coaching:** Individual guidance to improve skills and performance.
2. **Mentoring:** Long-term advice and career support from an experienced person.
3. **Job Rotation:** Experience in different jobs or departments.
4. **Job Enlargement:** Increasing the number of tasks at a similar level of responsibility.
5. **Job Enrichment:** Increasing responsibility, decision-making authority, and meaningful work.
6. **Special Assignments:** Giving employees challenging projects to develop their abilities.
7. **Management Development Programmes:** Preparing employees for supervisory and managerial positions.
8. **Workshops and Seminars:** Developing knowledge and professional skills.

### Job Enlargement vs Job Enrichment

|Job Enlargement|Job Enrichment|
|---|---|
|Adds more tasks at a similar level.|Adds greater responsibility and authority.|
|Focuses on horizontal expansion of work.|Focuses on vertical expansion of work.|
|Example: A clerk handles additional types of records.|Example: A clerk gains authority to approve certain transactions.|

Employee development is a continuous process of improving employees' knowledge, abilities, attitudes, and overall capabilities for future responsibilities.

It focuses on long-term growth rather than only the current job.

### [[Objectives of Development]]

1. To prepare employees for higher positions.
2. To develop leadership qualities.
3. To improve decision-making skills.
4. To encourage creativity and innovation.
5. To support career growth.
6. To prepare employees for organizational changes.
7. To develop managerial abilities.
8. To improve long-term organizational performance.

### [[Importance of Development]]

- Prepares employees for promotion.
- Develops future managers and leaders.
- Improves problem-solving abilities.
- Encourages continuous learning.
- Supports career planning.
- Helps organizations manage future challenges.

### Example

A company provides leadership development programmes to employees who may become managers in the future.

### [[Training vs Development]]

|Training|Development|
|---|---|
|Focuses mainly on the present job.|Focuses on future responsibilities.|
|Usually short-term and job-oriented.|Usually long-term and career-oriented.|
|Improves specific job skills.|Develops broader capabilities.|
|Often provided to improve immediate performance.|Often prepares employees for higher positions.|
|Example: Learning accounting software.|Example: Leadership development.|

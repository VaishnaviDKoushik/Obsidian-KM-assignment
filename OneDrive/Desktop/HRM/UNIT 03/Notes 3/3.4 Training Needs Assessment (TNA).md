### Meaning

Training Needs Assessment is the systematic process of identifying the gap between the skills employees currently possess and the skills required to perform their jobs effectively.

It helps an organization determine whether training is needed and what type of training should be provided.

[[### Objectives of Training Needs Assessment]]

1. Identify skill gaps.
2. Determine employees' training requirements.
3. Improve job performance.
4. Avoid unnecessary training expenses.
5. Identify future skill requirements.
6. Support organizational objectives.
7. Develop suitable training programmes.
8. Evaluate whether training is necessary to solve a performance problem.

### [[Levels of Training Needs Assessment]]

**1. Organizational Analysis**

Identifies training requirements based on organizational goals, resources, technology, and future plans.

Example: A company introduces new software and identifies the need to train its employees.

**2. Task Analysis**

Examines the duties of a job and identifies the knowledge and skills required to perform those duties.

Example: A computer operator must learn data entry, file management, and software operations.

**3. Individual Analysis**

Identifies the training needs of individual employees by examining their skills and performance.

Example: An employee who struggles with spreadsheet formulas receives additional training.

### [[Methods of Identifying Training Needs]]

1. Performance appraisal reports.
2. Interviews with employees and managers.
3. Questionnaires and surveys.
4. Observation of employees at work.
5. Skills tests.
6. Customer complaints and feedback.
7. Error and accident records.
8. Changes in technology or job responsibilities.

### [[Steps in Training Needs Assessment]]

1. Understand organizational objectives.
2. Analyze jobs and required competencies.
3. Evaluate employees' existing skills.
4. Identify gaps between required and existing skills.
5. Determine whether training can address those gaps.
6. Set training objectives.
7. Decide the training method and priority.

### Example

A company notices that employees frequently make errors while preparing reports. After assessing their skills, the HR department finds that they need spreadsheet training and arranges a suitable programme.

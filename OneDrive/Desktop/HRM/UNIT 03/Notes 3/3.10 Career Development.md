### Meaning

Career development is the continuous process through which employees identify career goals and develop the skills, knowledge, and experience needed to achieve them.

It involves both the employee's efforts and the organization's support.

### [[Objectives of Career Development]]

1. Help employees achieve career goals.
2. Identify future career opportunities.
3. Develop professional skills.
4. Prepare employees for higher positions.
5. Improve employee motivation.
6. Support succession planning.
7. Retain talented employees.
8. Match employee aspirations with organizational needs.

### [[Steps in Career Development]]

**Step 1: Self-Assessment**

Identify personal interests, strengths, weaknesses, and abilities.

**Step 2: Set Career Goals**

Decide the desired job role or career direction.

**Step 3: Identify Skill Gaps**

Determine which qualifications, skills, or experience are still needed.

**Step 4: Prepare a Career Plan**

Identify courses, training, projects, and work experiences that support career goals.

**Step 5: Develop Skills**

Participate in training, mentoring, education, and practical work.

**Step 6: Review Progress**

Evaluate progress and adjust career goals when necessary.

### [[Methods of Career Development]]

1. Training and development programmes.
2. Mentoring.
3. Coaching.
4. Job rotation.
5. Promotion opportunities.
6. Special projects.
7. Further education and professional certification.
8. Career counselling.
9. Succession planning.

### [[Importance of Career Development]]

- Improves employee motivation.
- Helps employees achieve professional goals.
- Builds future leadership capability.
- Encourages employee retention.
- Supports organizational growth.
- Improves employee engagement.

### [[Training vs Career Development]]

Training focuses mainly on improving job-related skills, while career development focuses on long-term professional growth and future opportunities.

### Example

A software developer who wants to become a team leader develops communication, project management, and leadership skills through training and mentoring.

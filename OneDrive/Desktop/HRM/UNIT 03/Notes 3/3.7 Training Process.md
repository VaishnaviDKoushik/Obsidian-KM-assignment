The training process consists of the steps used to plan, conduct, and evaluate a training programme.

### [[Steps in the Training Process]]

**Step 1: Identify Training Needs**

Determine the gap between employees' current skills and required skills.

**Step 2: Set Training Objectives**

Decide what employees should know or be able to do after training.

**Step 3: Design the Training Programme**

Choose the content, trainer, duration, materials, and training method.

**Step 4: Implement Training**

Conduct the training through suitable activities and learning methods.

**Step 5: Evaluate Training**

Check whether employees have learned the required skills and whether performance has improved.

**Step 6: Follow-Up**

Provide further guidance or training if necessary.

### Example

A company identifies that employees lack presentation skills, sets a training objective, conducts a communication workshop, and evaluates employees through practice presentations.

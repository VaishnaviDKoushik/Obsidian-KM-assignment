### Meaning

Job evaluation is the systematic process of determining the relative worth of different jobs within an organization to establish a fair and consistent pay structure.

Job evaluation assesses the job itself, not the personal performance of the employee holding it.

### [[Objectives of Job Evaluation]]

1. Establish a fair salary structure.
2. Maintain internal pay equity.
3. Compare the relative value of jobs.
4. Reduce wage-related disputes.
5. Support compensation decisions.
6. Create consistency in pay administration.
7. Help maintain logical salary grades.

### [[Methods of Job Evaluation]]

**1. Ranking Method**

Jobs are arranged from highest to lowest according to their overall relative worth.

- Advantage: Simple and inexpensive.
- Limitation: May be subjective and less suitable for large organizations.

**2. Job Classification Method**

Jobs are grouped into predetermined classes or grades according to their responsibilities and requirements.

- Advantage: Easy to understand and administer.
- Limitation: Some jobs may not fit neatly into a particular grade.

**3. Point Rating Method**

Jobs are evaluated using factors such as skill, effort, responsibility, and working conditions. Points are assigned to each factor.

- Advantage: Systematic and detailed.
- Limitation: Requires time and careful design.

**4. Factor Comparison Method**

Jobs are compared using selected compensable factors, and monetary values or pay relationships are assigned to those factors.

- Advantage: Allows detailed comparisons.
- Limitation: Complex to design and maintain.

### [[Importance of Job Evaluation]]

- Helps establish fair pay relationships.
- Reduces arbitrary salary decisions.
- Supports compensation planning.
- Improves transparency in job grading.
- Helps employees understand differences between job levels.

### [[Job Evaluation vs Performance Appraisal]]

|Job Evaluation|Performance Appraisal|
|---|---|
|Evaluates the relative worth of a job.|Evaluates an employee's job performance.|
|Supports job grading and pay structures.|Supports feedback, development, and performance decisions.|
|Focuses on job requirements.|Focuses on the employee's performance.|

### Meaning

Recruitment is the process of identifying, attracting, and encouraging suitable candidates to apply for available jobs in an organization.

Recruitment is generally called a positive process because it attracts candidates to apply.

### [[Objectives of Recruitment]]

1. Attract qualified candidates.
2. Fill existing vacancies.
3. Meet future manpower requirements.
4. Find candidates with suitable skills.
5. Support organizational growth.
6. Improve workforce quality.
7. Control recruitment costs.
8. Build a positive employer image.

### [[Characteristics of Recruitment]]

1. It attracts potential candidates.
2. It takes place before selection.
3. It is a continuous activity in many organizations.
4. It involves communicating job opportunities.
5. It connects organizations with the labour market.

### [[Recruitment Process]]

**Step 1: Identify the Vacancy**

Determine the job title, number of employees required, department, and location.

**Step 2: Conduct Job Analysis**

Identify the duties, responsibilities, skills, and qualifications required.

**Step 3: Prepare Job Description and Job Specification**

Explain the job and the qualifications required from the candidate.

**Step 4: Choose Recruitment Sources**

Decide whether to recruit internally or externally.

**Step 5: Advertise the Vacancy**

Communicate the job opportunity through suitable channels.

**Step 6: Receive Applications**

Collect applications and resumes from interested candidates.

**Step 7: Create the Applicant Pool**

Prepare a group of candidates who can be considered for selection.

### Example

A company requires a marketing executive. It advertises the vacancy on job portals and its career website and receives applications from interested candidates.

### Meaning

Selection is the process of evaluating applicants and choosing the candidate who is most suitable for a particular job.

Selection is generally called a negative process in traditional HR terminology because unsuitable applicants are eliminated during the process.

### [[Objectives of Selection]]

1. Choose qualified candidates.
2. Match candidates with job requirements.
3. Improve employee performance.
4. Reduce employee turnover.
5. Avoid unsuitable hiring decisions.
6. Ensure fair evaluation of applicants.

### [[Steps in the Selection Process]]

**Step 1: Screening Applications**

Check whether applicants meet the basic requirements.

**Step 2: Preliminary Interview**

Conduct an initial discussion to assess suitability and interest.

**Step 3: Employment Tests**

Assess knowledge, aptitude, technical ability, personality, or other job-related qualities.

**Step 4: Main Interview**

Evaluate the candidate's qualifications, experience, communication, and job suitability.

**Step 5: Reference and Background Checks**

Verify relevant qualifications, experience, and references, with appropriate consent and in accordance with applicable requirements.

**Step 6: Medical Examination, Where Relevant**

For jobs where necessary and lawful, assess fitness for specific job requirements while respecting privacy and applicable law.

**Step 7: Final Selection**

Compare the candidates and choose the most suitable applicant.

**Step 8: Job Offer**

Communicate the employment offer and relevant terms.

**Step 9: Appointment**

Complete the required employment documentation.

### [[Common Selection Tests]]

1. **Aptitude Test:** Measures potential to learn or perform certain tasks.
2. **Achievement Test:** Measures knowledge or skills already acquired.
3. **Personality Test:** Assesses relevant personality characteristics.
4. **Intelligence or Cognitive Ability Test:** Assesses reasoning and problem-solving abilities.
5. **Technical Test:** Evaluates job-specific technical skills.

### [[Importance of Selection]]

- Improves employee quality.
- Reduces the risk of poor hiring.
- Helps place suitable people in suitable jobs.
- Saves time and costs associated with repeated recruitment.
- Supports organizational performance.

### [[Recruitment vs Selection]]

|Recruitment|Selection|
|---|---|
|Attracts applicants.|Chooses the suitable applicant.|
|Creates an applicant pool.|Evaluates and filters applicants.|
|Generally a positive process.|Generally a negative process in traditional terminology.|
|Comes first.|Follows recruitment.|

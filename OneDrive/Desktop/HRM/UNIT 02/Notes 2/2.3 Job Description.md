### [[Meaning]]

A job description is a written statement explaining the duties, responsibilities, authority, and working conditions of a particular job.

In simple words, a job description explains the job.

[[### Contents of a Job Description]]

1. Job title
2. Job location
3. Department
4. Reporting relationship
5. Job summary
6. Duties and responsibilities
7. Authority
8. Working conditions
9. Performance expectations

[[Importance of Job Description]]

1. Clarifies employee duties.
2. Helps prepare recruitment advertisements.
3. Supports candidate selection.
4. Provides a basis for performance appraisal.
5. Helps identify training requirements.
6. Reduces confusion and duplication of work.
7. Helps employees understand their responsibilities.

### Example

A job description for an HR Executive may include maintaining employee records, assisting in recruitment, managing attendance, and supporting training programmes.

[[### Job Description vs Job Specification]]

|Job Description|Job Specification|
|---|---|
|Describes the job.|Describes the person required.|
|Focuses on duties and responsibilities.|Focuses on qualifications and skills.|
|Explains what work must be done.|Explains who can perform the work.|
|Example: Preparing employee reports.|Example: Degree and communication skills.|

**Memory trick:** Job Description = Job; Job Specification = Person.

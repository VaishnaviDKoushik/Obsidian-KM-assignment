
##[[ 1. Introduction]]

Human resources are one of the most important resources of an organization. Employees use their knowledge, skills, and abilities to achieve organizational goals.

Meeting human resource requirements means identifying the number and type of employees needed, attracting suitable candidates, selecting the right people, and placing them in appropriate jobs.
### Main Activities

1. Job Analysis
2. Job Description
3. Human Resource Planning
4. Recruitment
5. Sources of Recruitment
6. Selection Process
7. Interview
8. Placement
9. Induction
10. Separation
11. Job Evaluation

### Human Resource Requirement Process

Job Analysis → Job Description → Human Resource Planning → Recruitment → Selection → Interview and Assessment → Placement → Induction → Employee Development and Retention → Separation

### Meaning

Induction is the process of introducing a newly appointed employee to the organization, its policies, rules, colleagues, and job responsibilities.

It is also known as employee orientation.

### [[Objectives of Induction]]

1. Welcome the new employee.
2. Introduce organizational policies and rules.
3. Explain duties and responsibilities.
4. Familiarize the employee with colleagues and supervisors.
5. Explain workplace safety procedures.
6. Reduce anxiety and confusion.
7. Help the employee adjust to the new workplace.
8. Improve confidence and job satisfaction.

[[### Induction Process]]

**Step 1: Welcome the Employee**

Introduce the employee to the organization and HR department.

**Step 2: Explain the Organization**

Provide information about the organization's history, objectives, structure, and culture.

**Step 3: Explain Policies**

Discuss attendance, leave, working hours, code of conduct, and other relevant policies.

**Step 4: Introduce Colleagues**

Introduce the employee to team members, supervisors, and relevant departments.

**Step 5: Explain the Job**

Describe duties, responsibilities, performance expectations, and reporting relationships.

**Step 6: Explain Safety and Facilities**

Provide necessary safety instructions and explain workplace facilities.

**Step 7: Follow Up**

Check whether the employee understands the job and requires further assistance.

### Advantages of Induction

- Reduces employee anxiety.
- Helps employees adjust quickly.
- Clarifies job expectations.
- Promotes good workplace relationships.
- Improves confidence and productivity.
- Helps employees understand organizational rules.

### Example

On the first day, a company explains its policies to a new employee, introduces the team, provides login credentials, and explains the assigned work.

Recruitment sources are the methods used by organizations to find potential employees.

There are two main sources.

[[A. Internal Sources of Recruitment]]

Internal recruitment means filling vacancies using existing employees of the organization.

**Methods:**

1. **Promotion:** Moving an employee to a higher position.
2. **Transfer:** Moving an employee to another department or location, usually at a similar level.
3. **Internal Job Posting:** Informing existing employees about vacancies.
4. **Employee Skills Inventory:** Identifying current employees who have the required skills.

**Advantages**

- Lower recruitment costs.
- Saves time.
- Motivates existing employees.
- Employees already know the organization.
- Helps employee career development.

**Disadvantages**

- Limited number of candidates.
- May create competition among employees.
- Can reduce the introduction of new ideas.
- Another vacancy may arise when an employee is promoted or transferred.

### [[B. External Sources of Recruitment]]

External recruitment means hiring candidates from outside the organization.

**Methods:**

1. **Advertisements:** Newspapers, websites, and social media.
2. **Campus Recruitment:** Hiring students from colleges and universities.
3. **Employment Exchanges:** Using employment services to find candidates.
4. **Recruitment Agencies:** Obtaining candidates through professional agencies.
5. **Online Job Portals:** Publishing vacancies on online employment platforms.
6. **Employee Referrals:** Existing employees recommend potential candidates.
7. **Walk-in Interviews:** Candidates attend interviews without a prior individual appointment.
8. **Direct Applications:** Candidates submit resumes directly to the organization.

**Advantages**

- Provides access to a larger talent pool.
- Introduces new ideas and skills.
- Offers opportunities to find specialized talent.
- Supports organizational expansion.

**Disadvantages**

- Can be expensive.
- Takes time to advertise and assess candidates.
- Candidates may not be familiar with the organization.
- A suitable candidate is not guaranteed.

### [[Internal vs External Recruitment]]

|Internal Recruitment|External Recruitment|
|---|---|
|Uses existing employees.|Uses candidates from outside.|
|Usually costs less.|May cost more.|
|Can improve employee morale.|Can introduce new ideas.|
|Candidate pool is limited.|Candidate pool is wider.|
|Example: Promotion.|Example: Campus recruitment.|

### Meaning

An interview is a formal conversation between an interviewer and a candidate to collect information and assess suitability for a job.

### [[Objectives of an Interview]]

1. Assess the candidate's knowledge.
2. Understand qualifications and experience.
3. Evaluate communication skills.
4. Assess problem-solving ability.
5. Understand career interests and motivation.
6. Clarify information in the application.
7. Determine suitability for the position.

### [[Types of Interviews]]

**1. Structured Interview:** Every candidate is asked a predetermined set of questions.

**2. Unstructured Interview:** Questions are flexible and depend on the conversation.

**3. Semi-Structured Interview:** Uses prepared questions with additional follow-up questions.

**4. Panel Interview:** Several interviewers assess one candidate.

**5. Group Interview:** Several candidates are assessed together.

**6. Technical Interview:** Evaluates technical knowledge and job-specific skills.

**7. Behavioural Interview:** Asks about past experiences to understand how candidates may behave in similar situations.

**8. Online Interview:** Conducted through video-conferencing platforms.

### Advantages of Interviews

- Allows direct communication.
- Helps clarify application details.
- Provides information about communication skills.
- Enables job-related questions and follow-ups.

### Limitations of Interviews

- Personal bias may affect decisions.
- Interviews can be time-consuming.
- Nervousness may affect candidate performance.
- Interviewers may interpret answers differently.

### [[Qualities of a Good Interviewer]]

1. Good communication skills.
2. Knowledge of the job.
3. Fair and unbiased judgment.
4. Active listening.
5. Ability to ask relevant questions.
6. Professional behaviour.

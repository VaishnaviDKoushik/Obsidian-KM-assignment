### Meaning

Placement is the process of assigning a selected employee to a suitable job, department, and work location.

### [[Objectives of Placement]]

1. Match employees with suitable jobs.
2. Use employee skills effectively.
3. Help employees understand their responsibilities.
4. Improve job satisfaction.
5. Reduce early employee turnover.
6. Support organizational efficiency.

### [[Steps in Placement]]

1. Review the employee's qualifications and skills.
2. Identify a suitable position.
3. Assign duties and responsibilities.
4. Inform the employee about reporting relationships.
5. Explain work location and working conditions.
6. Provide necessary resources and instructions.
7. Monitor initial performance and provide support.

### [[Importance of Placement]]

- Improves productivity.
- Reduces confusion about responsibilities.
- Helps employees adjust to their jobs.
- Encourages job satisfaction.
- Supports effective use of human resources.

### Example

A newly selected computer science graduate with suitable programming skills is placed in the software development department.

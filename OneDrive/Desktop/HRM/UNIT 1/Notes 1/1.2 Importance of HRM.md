[[1.2Importance of HRM]]
1. **Recruitment:** Helps the organization find suitable candidates for jobs.
2. **Training:** Improves employees' knowledge and skills.
3. **Motivation:** Encourages employees to work effectively.
4. **Performance appraisal:** Evaluates employee performance.
5. **Compensation:** Ensures employees receive appropriate salaries and benefits.
6. **Employee relations:** Maintains good relationships between employees and management.
7. **Organizational growth:** Helps the organization achieve its goals through effective use of human resources.

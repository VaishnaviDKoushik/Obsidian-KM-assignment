### [[1. Traditional Perspective]]

The traditional perspective focuses mainly on employee administration, attendance, salaries, rules, and maintaining records.

**Example:** Maintaining employee attendance and salary records.

### [[2. Human Relations Perspective]]

This perspective emphasizes employee satisfaction, communication, teamwork, and good relationships between employees and management.

**Example:** Conducting team meetings and listening to employee concerns.

### [[3. Strategic Perspective]]

The strategic perspective connects HR policies and practices with the long-term goals of the organization.

**Example:** Training employees in new technologies to support company growth.

### [[4. Employee Development Perspective]]

This perspective focuses on improving employees' skills, knowledge, and career growth.

**Example:** Providing leadership training to employees.


The functions of HRM are the activities performed to manage employees effectively and achieve organizational goals.

## 1. Managerial Functions

**Planning:** Deciding the human resources required for the future.

**Organizing:** Assigning duties and responsibilities to employees.

**Directing:** Guiding, motivating, and leading employees.

**Controlling:** Checking employee performance and correcting mistakes.

## 2. Operative Functions

**Procurement:** Recruiting and selecting suitable employees.

**Development:** Providing training to improve employee skills.

**Compensation:** Providing salaries, wages, bonuses, and benefits.

**Integration:** Maintaining good relationships between employees and management.

**Maintenance:** Ensuring employee health, safety, and welfare.

**Separation:** Managing retirement, resignation, and termination.

Human Resource Management (HRM) aims to manage employees effectively and achieve the goals of an organization. It ensures that the right people are recruited, trained, motivated, and retained.

## [Objectives of HRM]

[[1. **Organizational Objectives:]]** To help the organization achieve its goals by using human resources effectively.
    
[[2. **Functional Objectives:]]** To maintain an appropriate HR department that meets the organization's needs.
    
[[3. **Personal Objectives:**]] To support employees in achieving personal goals, job satisfaction, and career growth.
    
[[4. **Social Objectives:**]] To fulfil social responsibilities and follow labour laws and ethical practices.
    
[[5. **Recruitment and Selection:**]] To appoint suitable candidates for the right jobs.
    
[[6. **Training and Development:** ]]To improve employees' knowledge, skills, and abilities.
    
[[7. **Employee Motivation:**]] To encourage employees through fair wages, rewards, recognition, and benefits.
    
[[8. **Employee Retention:**]] To retain talented employees and reduce employee turnover.

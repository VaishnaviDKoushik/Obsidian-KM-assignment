## 1.1 Meaning of Human Resource Management

Human Resource Management (HRM) is the process of managing people in an organization. It includes recruiting, selecting, training, motivating, evaluating, and retaining employees to achieve organizational goals.

Employees are important assets because their knowledge, skills, and abilities help an organization succeed.

**Example:** A college appoints teachers, provides training, evaluates their performance, and pays their salaries. These activities are part of HRM.

## [[1.2 Definition of HRM]]

Human Resource Management is the systematic process of acquiring, developing, motivating, and maintaining employees to achieve individual and organizational objectives.

Human Resource Management has developed gradually as organizations have changed the way they manage employees.

### [[1. Industrial Revolution]]

Factories increased the demand for workers. Employee supervision, working hours, wages, and working conditions became important concerns.

### [[2. Personnel Management]]

Organizations began maintaining employee records, managing wages, handling recruitment, and addressing employee welfare.

### [[3. Human Relations Movement]]

Greater attention was given to employee motivation, job satisfaction, communication, and relationships at work.

### [[4. Human Resource Management]]

Employee recruitment, selection, training, compensation, and performance management became organized management activities.

### [[5. Strategic HRM]]

HR practices became more closely connected with business objectives, workforce planning, employee development, and organizational performance.

HRM is necessary because employees are essential for the effective functioning of every organization.

###  [[1. To recruit suitable employees]]

HRM helps identify and hire candidates who match job requirements.

### [[2. To improve employee performance]]

Training and performance appraisal help employees improve their work.

### [[3. To maintain discipline]]

HR policies establish workplace rules and expected behaviour.

### [[4. To provide fair compensation]]

HRM helps manage salaries, incentives, and employee benefits.

### [[5. To improve employee relations]]

HRM helps resolve workplace problems and maintain cooperation.

### [[6. To manage organizational changes]]

HRM helps employees adapt to new technology, policies, and working methods.

### [[7. To comply with employment laws]]

HRM helps the organization follow applicable labour and employment requirements.

The main purpose of HRM is to manage human resources effectively and help the organization achieve its goals.

The important purposes are:

1. **Achieving organizational goals:** Ensures employees contribute to the success of the organization.
2. **Hiring suitable employees:** Selects people with the required qualifications and skills.
3. **Developing employees:** Improves employee knowledge and abilities through training.
4. **Motivating employees:** Encourages employees to perform better.
5. **Maintaining employee satisfaction:** Promotes a positive working environment.
6. **Using resources effectively:** Ensures employees' skills are used efficiently.
7. **Supporting employee growth:** Helps employees develop their careers.

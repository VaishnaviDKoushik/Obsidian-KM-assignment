[[1. Planning]]

Planning means deciding in advance what needs to be done and how to do it.

**Example:** Estimating how many employees a company will need next year.

### [[2. Organizing]]

Organizing means arranging resources, assigning duties, and establishing responsibilities.

**Example:** Assigning employees to the HR, finance, and marketing departments.

### [[3. Staffing]]

Staffing means finding, selecting, training, and placing suitable employees in jobs.

**Example:** Recruiting a software developer for a company.

### [[4. Directing]]

Directing means guiding, supervising, and motivating employees to perform their work.

**Example:** A manager guides employees to complete a project.

### [[5. Controlling]]

Controlling means comparing actual performance with planned targets and correcting deviations.

**Example:** Reviewing employee performance and taking corrective action when targets are not met.

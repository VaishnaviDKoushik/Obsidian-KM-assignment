Difference between Personal and Human resource managemenet:

| Personnel Management                                    | Human Resource Management                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Traditionally focuses on employee administration.       | Focuses on managing and developing employees.             |
| Often emphasizes routine personnel activities.          | Connects HR practices with organizational objectives.     |
| Training may be treated as a separate activity.         | Employee development is an important responsibility.      |
| Mainly associated with traditional personnel functions. | Includes both operational and strategic responsibilities. |

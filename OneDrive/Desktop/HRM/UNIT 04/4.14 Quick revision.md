
- **Motivation:** Encourages people to work towards goals.
- **Maslow:** Five levels of human needs.
- **Herzberg:** Hygiene factors and motivators.
- **McGregor:** Theory X and Theory Y.
- **McClelland:** Achievement, affiliation and power.
- **Vroom:** Expectancy, instrumentality and valence.
- **Leadership:** Influencing and guiding people.
- **Autocratic:** Leader makes decisions.
- **Democratic:** Employees participate in decisions.
- **Laissez-faire:** Employees receive considerable freedom.
- **Promotion:** Movement to a higher position.
- **Transfer:** Movement to another job or location, usually at a similar level.
- **Induction:** Introduction of new employees.
- **Employee morale:** Overall confidence and enthusiasm.
- **Employee attrition:** Employees leave and are not immediately replaced.
- **Deviant workplace behaviour:** Conduct that violates important workplace standards.

### Importance of Motivation

1. **Improves productivity:** Motivated employees work efficiently.
2. **Increases job satisfaction:** Employees feel happy and valued.
3. **Improves morale:** Employees develop a positive attitude.
4. **Encourages teamwork:** Employees cooperate with colleagues.
5. **Reduces employee turnover:** Employees are more likely to remain in the organization.
6. **Achieves organizational goals:** Employees make greater efforts to complete their work.

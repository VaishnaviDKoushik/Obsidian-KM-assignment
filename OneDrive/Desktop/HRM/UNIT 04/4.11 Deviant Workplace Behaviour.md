Deviant workplace behaviour refers to intentional behaviour that violates important organizational rules or accepted workplace standards and can harm the organization or its members.

### [[Examples]]

1. Theft of company property.
2. Workplace bullying.
3. Harassment.
4. Deliberate damage to equipment.
5. Repeated intentional violation of safety rules.
6. Misuse of confidential information.
7. Deliberate sabotage of work.

### [[Causes]]

- Workplace stress.
- Unfair treatment.
- Poor supervision.
- Workplace conflict.
- Lack of accountability.
- Dissatisfaction and low morale.

### [[Prevention]]

1. Establish clear workplace policies.
2. Treat employees fairly.
3. Provide appropriate training.
4. Encourage employees to report concerns safely.
5. Investigate complaints fairly.
6. Apply disciplinary procedures consistently.
7. Promote respectful workplace relationships.

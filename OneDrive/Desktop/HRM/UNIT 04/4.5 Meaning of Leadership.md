Leadership is the ability to influence, guide and inspire people to work towards common goals.

A leader provides direction, motivates employees and helps the team achieve organizational objectives.

**Example:** A team leader assigns tasks, resolves problems and encourages team members to complete a project.

### [[Importance of Leadership]]

1. Provides direction to employees.
2. Motivates employees to perform better.
3. Improves communication.
4. Encourages teamwork.
5. Helps solve workplace problems.
6. Supports decision-making.
7. Helps manage organizational change.

### Qualities of a Good Leader

1. Good communication skills.
2. Confidence.
3. Honesty and integrity.
4. Decision-making ability.
5. Problem-solving skills.
6. Empathy.
7. Accountability.
8. Ability to motivate others.

Employee attrition refers to the reduction in the number of employees when people leave an organization and their positions are not immediately replaced.

Employees may leave because of resignation, retirement or other reasons.

### [[Causes of Employee Attrition]]

1. Low salary and benefits.
2. Limited career opportunities.
3. Poor management.
4. Lack of recognition.
5. Excessive workload.
6. Poor work-life balance.
7. Unhealthy workplace culture.
8. Better opportunities elsewhere.

### [[Effects of Employee Attrition]]

1. Loss of experienced employees.
2. Increased recruitment costs.
3. Additional training costs.
4. Temporary reduction in productivity.
5. Increased workload for remaining employees.
6. Loss of organizational knowledge.

### [[Ways to Reduce Employee Attrition]]

1. Provide fair compensation.
2. Offer career development opportunities.
3. Recognize employee achievements.
4. Encourage work-life balance.
5. Improve leadership and communication.
6. Maintain a safe and respectful workplace.
7. Conduct exit interviews to understand why employees leave.

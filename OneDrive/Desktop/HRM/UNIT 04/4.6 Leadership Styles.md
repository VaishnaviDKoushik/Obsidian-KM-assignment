### [[1. Autocratic Leadership]]

The leader makes decisions independently and expects employees to follow instructions.

**Advantages:** Quick decisions and clear direction.

**Disadvantages:** Limited employee participation and possible low morale.

**Example:** A supervisor makes an immediate decision during an emergency.

### [[2. Democratic Leadership]]

The leader consults employees and encourages participation in decision-making.

**Advantages:** Better teamwork, creativity and employee involvement.

**Disadvantages:** Decisions may take more time.

**Example:** A manager asks team members for suggestions before selecting a project plan.

### [[3. Laissez-Faire Leadership]]

The leader gives employees considerable freedom to make decisions and manage their work.

**Advantages:** Encourages independence and creativity.

**Disadvantages:** May cause confusion if employees lack experience or direction.

**Example:** An experienced software team is allowed to decide how to complete a project.

### [[4. Transformational Leadership]]

The leader inspires employees with a vision and encourages innovation and improvement.

**Example:** A manager motivates a team to adopt new technology and develop better services.

### [[5. Transactional Leadership]]

The leader emphasizes clear targets, rules, rewards and consequences.

**Example:** Employees receive incentives for achieving agreed performance targets.

### [[6. Situational Leadership]]

The leader changes their approach depending on the task, situation and employees' abilities.

**Example:** A new employee receives close guidance, while an experienced employee receives greater independence.

### [[Difference]] Between Autocratic and Democratic Leadership

|Autocratic Leadership|Democratic Leadership|
|---|---|
|Leader makes most decisions.|Employees participate in decisions.|
|Limited consultation.|Encourages consultation.|
|Decisions are often quick.|Decisions may take longer.|
|Useful in urgent situations.|Useful when team input is valuable.|
|May reduce employee involvement.|Often increases employee involvement.|

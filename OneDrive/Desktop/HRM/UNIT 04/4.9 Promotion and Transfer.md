### [[Promotion]]

Promotion is the movement of an employee to a higher position with greater responsibility and usually higher pay or status.

**Example:** An HR Executive becomes an HR Manager.

**Importance of Promotion**

1. Motivates employees.
2. Recognizes good performance.
3. Provides career growth.
4. Helps retain talented employees.

### [[Transfer]]

Transfer is the movement of an employee from one job, department, location or shift to another, usually at a similar level.

**Example:** An employee is moved from the Mysuru branch to the Bengaluru branch in a similar position.

**Reasons for Transfer**

1. Organizational requirements.
2. Employee requests.
3. Balancing staff between departments.
4. Developing employee experience.
5. Addressing workplace or staffing needs.

### [[Difference]] Between Promotion and Transfer

|Promotion|Transfer|
|---|---|
|Usually involves a higher position.|Usually involves a similar-level position.|
|Often increases responsibility.|Changes job, department, location or shift.|
|Usually increases status.|Usually does not change status.|
|Often includes higher pay.|Pay may remain unchanged.|

Induction is the process of introducing a new employee to the organization, workplace, colleagues, policies and job responsibilities.

### [[Objectives of Induction]]

1. Welcome new employees.
2. Explain organizational rules and policies.
3. Introduce colleagues and supervisors.
4. Explain job duties.
5. Communicate safety procedures.
6. Reduce fear and confusion.
7. Help employees adjust quickly.

### [[Steps in Induction]]

1. Welcome the employee.
2. Explain the organization and its objectives.
3. Introduce the employee to the team.
4. Explain workplace policies and procedures.
5. Explain job duties and expectations.
6. Provide necessary tools and access.
7. Follow up to answer questions.

**Example:** A newly appointed employee receives an introduction to the company, meets the team and learns how to use workplace systems.

### [[1. Intrinsic Motivation]]

Intrinsic motivation comes from within a person.

**Example:** An employee learns a new skill because they enjoy learning.

### [[2. Extrinsic Motivation]]

Extrinsic motivation comes from external rewards or consequences.

**Example:** An employee works hard to receive a bonus or promotion.

### [[3. Positive Motivation]]

Positive motivation encourages employees through rewards, appreciation and recognition.

**Example:** Giving an award to an employee for excellent performance.

### [[4. Negative Motivation]]

Negative motivation uses the possibility of punishment or undesirable consequences to influence behaviour.

**Example:** An employee follows safety rules to avoid disciplinary action.

Positive motivation generally supports a more encouraging work environment, while negative motivation must be used carefully and fairly.

### 1.[[ Digital HR and Automation]]

Organizations use HR software to manage recruitment, attendance, payroll, and employee records.

**Influence:** Saves time, reduces manual work, and improves access to HR information.

### 2. [[Artificial Intelligence in Recruitment]]

AI-based tools may assist with tasks such as organizing applications, matching skills to job requirements, and scheduling interviews.

**Influence:** Can improve efficiency, but organizations must monitor bias, protect personal data, and maintain appropriate human oversight.

### [[3. Remote and Hybrid Work]]

Employees may work from home, the office, or a combination of both.

**Influence:** HR must support communication, collaboration, productivity, and employee well-being across locations.

### [[4. Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion]]

Organizations increasingly focus on fair treatment and opportunities for people with different backgrounds and abilities.

**Influence:** Can improve workplace fairness, belonging, and access to talent.

### [[5. Employee Well-Being]]

Organizations pay greater attention to mental well-being, work-life balance, and healthy working conditions.

**Influence:** Can support engagement, satisfaction, and retention.

### [[6. Continuous Learning]]

Employees need to update their skills as technologies and job requirements change.

**Influence:** HR develops training programmes, reskilling opportunities, and career development plans.

### [[7. Data-Driven HR]]

HR departments analyse recruitment, performance, attendance, and attrition data.

**Influence:** Helps identify trends and supports evidence-based decisions.

### [[8. Global Talent Management]]

Organizations recruit and manage employees across different countries and cultures.

**Influence:** HR must consider cultural differences, local employment laws, time zones, and international workforce requirements.

Human Resource Management continues to change due to technology, globalization, new employee expectations, and changes in business practices.

### [[A. Strategic Human Resource Management (SHRM)]]

**Meaning:** Strategic Human Resource Management is the process of aligning HR policies and practices with the long-term goals and strategies of an organization.

### [[Objectives of SHRM]]

1. To connect HR plans with business objectives.
    
2. To develop employee skills.
    
3. To improve organizational performance.
    
4. To attract and retain talented employees.
    
5. To prepare the workforce for future needs.
    
6. To create a competitive advantage.
    

### [[Features of SHRM]]

- Long-term planning.
    
- Workforce planning.
    
- Talent management.
    
- Employee development.
    
- Performance management.
    
- HR data analysis.
    
- Alignment between HR and business goals.
    

### Example

If a company plans to expand its online business, HR may recruit digital specialists, train existing employees, and develop new performance measures to support that strategy.

### Difference Between Traditional HRM and Strategic HRM

|Traditional HRM|Strategic HRM|
|---|---|
|Often focuses on routine HR administration.|Connects HR practices to long-term business goals.|
|Emphasizes immediate staffing and employee matters.|Emphasizes future workforce requirements.|
|May focus on separate HR activities.|Integrates recruitment, training, performance, and retention strategies.|
|Measures administrative efficiency.|Also considers contribution to organizational performance.|

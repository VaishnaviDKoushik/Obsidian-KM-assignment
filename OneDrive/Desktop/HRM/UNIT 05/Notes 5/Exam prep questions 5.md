# [[QUICK REVISION NOTES]]

- **Industrial Relations:** Relationships among employers, employees, unions, and government.
    
- **Industrial Dispute:** A disagreement related to employment or working conditions.
    
- **Negotiation:** Parties discuss directly to reach an agreement.
    
- **Collective Bargaining:** Employer and worker representatives negotiate employment conditions.
    
- **Conciliation:** A neutral person helps parties reach agreement.
    
- **Arbitration:** A neutral arbitrator gives a decision.
    
- **Trade Union:** An organization representing workers' interests.
    
- **Strike:** Employees collectively stop working.
    
- **Lockout:** Employer temporarily closes the workplace or refuses work in a dispute.
    
- **Maslow:** Physiological → Safety → Social → Esteem → Self-Actualization.
    
- **Incentive Theory:** Rewards and incentives can encourage behaviour.
    
- **Herzberg:** Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators encourage satisfaction.
    
- **SHRM:** Aligning HR practices with long-term business strategy.
    
- **Global HR Trends:** Digital HR, AI, remote work, diversity, well-being, continuous learning, and data-driven decisions.
    

**Remember the dispute-settlement methods:** Negotiation → Conciliation/Mediation → Arbitration or Adjudication, depending on the dispute and applicable process.

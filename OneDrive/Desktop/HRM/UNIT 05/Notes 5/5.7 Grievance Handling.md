**Meaning:** A grievance is a complaint raised by an employee concerning workplace treatment, working conditions, policies, or employment rights.

### [[Common Causes]] of Grievances

- Salary problems.
    
- Unfair supervision.
    
- Workload issues.
    
- Discrimination or harassment.
    
- Leave disputes.
    
- Promotion disagreements.
    
- Unsafe working conditions.
    

### [[Steps]] in the Grievance Procedure

1. Receive the complaint.
    
2. Listen to the employee.
    
3. Collect relevant information.
    
4. Investigate fairly.
    
5. Discuss possible solutions.
    
6. Communicate the decision.
    
7. Allow an appeal where appropriate.
    
8. Follow up to check whether the problem is resolved.
    

### [[Importance]] of Grievance Handling

- Reduces workplace conflict.
    
- Improves employee trust.
    
- Promotes fair treatment.
    
- Prevents disputes from becoming more serious.
    
- Helps improve working conditions.

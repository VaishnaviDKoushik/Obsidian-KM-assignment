**Meaning:** Skill development is the process of improving employees' abilities and knowledge to perform their jobs effectively.

### [[Types of Skills]]

- Technical skills.
    
- Communication skills.
    
- Leadership skills.
    
- Teamwork skills.
    
- Problem-solving skills.
    
- Time-management skills.
    
- Interpersonal skills.
    

### [[Methods of Skill Development]]

- On-the-job training.
    
- Off-the-job training.
    
- Coaching and mentoring.
    
- Workshops.
    
- Online courses.
    
- Job rotation.
    
- Practical assignments.
    

### [[Importance of Skill Development]]

- Improves productivity.
    
- Reduces mistakes.
    
- Increases employee confidence.
    
- Prepares employees for promotions.
    
- Helps employees adapt to new technology.
    
- Supports organizational growth.

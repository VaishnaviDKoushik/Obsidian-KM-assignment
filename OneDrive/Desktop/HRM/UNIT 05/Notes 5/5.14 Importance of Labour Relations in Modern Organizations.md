Good labour relations benefit both employers and employees.

**[[Benefits to Employees]]:**

- Fair treatment.
    
- Better communication.
    
- Representation through unions where applicable.
    
- Improved employment conditions.
    
- Clear grievance procedures.
    

[[**Benefits to Employers]]:**

- Fewer workplace conflicts.
    
- Improved cooperation.
    
- More stable operations.
    
- Better communication with workers.
    
- Greater workforce confidence.
    

[[**Benefits to Society]]:**

- Supports industrial peace.
    
- Helps protect employment interests.
    
- Reduces disruption caused by disputes.
    
- Encourages stable economic activity.

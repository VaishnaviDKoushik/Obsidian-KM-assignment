### [[Strike]]

**Meaning:** A strike is a collective work stoppage by employees to express demands or protest employment conditions.

**Common causes:**

- Wage disputes.
    
- Poor working conditions.
    
- Unfair treatment.
    
- Disagreement over benefits.
    
- Failure to resolve grievances.
    

### [[Lockout]]

**Meaning:** A lockout is a temporary closure of a workplace or refusal by an employer to allow employees to work in the context of an industrial dispute, as defined by applicable labour law.

### [[Difference]] Between Strike and Lockout

|Strike|Lockout|
|---|---|
|Usually initiated by employees.|Initiated by the employer.|
|Employees stop working.|The employer temporarily closes the workplace or refuses work.|
|Often used to press employee demands.|Often used during an employer's dispute with workers.|

**Note:** The legality and requirements of strikes and lockouts depend on the applicable labour laws and circumstances.

Global trends influence how organizations recruit, train, motivate, evaluate, and retain employees.

|Global Trend|Influence on HR Practices|
|---|---|
|Digital HR|Use of HR software and automated processes.|
|AI in recruitment|Faster screening support and a need for bias monitoring.|
|Remote work|Flexible policies and digital communication.|
|Diversity and inclusion|Fairer recruitment and inclusive workplace policies.|
|Employee well-being|Well-being support and work-life balance initiatives.|
|Continuous learning|Reskilling and upskilling programmes.|
|Data-driven HR|Use of workforce data to support decisions.|
|Globalization|Cross-cultural training and international workforce planning.|

### [[Challenges]] of Global HR Trends

- High technology costs.
    
- Employee privacy concerns.
    
- Cybersecurity risks.
    
- Skills gaps.
    
- Cultural differences.
    
- Resistance to change.
    
- Differences in labour laws across countries.
    
- Risk of bias in automated decisions.
    

### How HR Can Respond

- Provide employee training.
    
- Protect personal information.
    
- Review HR technology for fairness.
    
- Communicate changes clearly.
    
- Follow applicable employment laws.
    
- Support employee well-being.
    
- Regularly evaluate HR policies.

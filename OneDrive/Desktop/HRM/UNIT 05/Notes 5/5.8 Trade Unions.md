**Meaning:** A trade union is an organization formed by workers to represent and protect their employment-related interests.

### [[Objectives]] of Trade Unions

1. To protect workers' rights.
    
2. To negotiate wages and benefits.
    
3. To improve working conditions.
    
4. To represent workers in disputes.
    
5. To promote job security.
    
6. To support employee welfare.
    
7. To participate in collective bargaining.
    

### [[Functions]] of Trade Unions

- Represent employees.
    
- Negotiate employment agreements.
    
- Raise workplace grievances.
    
- Provide advice and support.
    
- Promote health and safety.
    
- Communicate workers' concerns to management.
    

### Advantages of Trade Unions

- Give workers a collective voice.
    
- Support negotiation for better conditions.
    
- Help resolve employment disputes.
    
- Promote worker welfare.
    
- May improve communication with management.

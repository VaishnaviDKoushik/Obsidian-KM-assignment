Industrial relations and labour relations both deal with relationships between employers and workers.

**Industrial Relations:** A broad term covering relationships among employers, employees, trade unions, and government.

**Labour Relations:** Often focuses more specifically on the relationship between employers and workers or their representatives, particularly concerning employment conditions and collective bargaining.

### [[Difference]] Between Industrial Relations and Labour Relations

|Industrial Relations|Labour Relations|
|---|---|
|Covers relationships among several parties in the industrial environment.|Often focuses on employer-worker relationships.|
|Includes employers, employees, unions, and government.|Often emphasizes workers and their representatives.|
|Covers cooperation, disputes, policies, and workplace relations.|Often emphasizes wages, working conditions, grievances, and bargaining.|

The terms sometimes overlap and may be used interchangeably.

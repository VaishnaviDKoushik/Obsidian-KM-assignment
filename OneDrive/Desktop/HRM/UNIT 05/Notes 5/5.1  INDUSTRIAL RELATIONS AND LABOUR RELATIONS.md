## [[1. Introduction to Industrial Relations]]

**Meaning:** Industrial Relations (IR) refers to the relationship between employers, employees, trade unions, and the government in an industrial or organizational environment.

Industrial relations aim to maintain peaceful relationships between management and workers and resolve workplace problems.

### [[Objectives of Industrial Relations]]

1. To maintain good relationships between employers and employees.
    
2. To prevent industrial disputes.
    
3. To protect workers' rights and interests.
    
4. To improve working conditions.
    
5. To promote cooperation between management and workers.
    
6. To improve productivity.
    
7. To encourage collective bargaining.
    
8. To maintain industrial peace.
    

[[### Importance of Industrial Relations]]

- Promotes a peaceful working environment.
    
- Reduces conflicts between employers and employees.
    
- Improves employee morale.
    
- Helps resolve complaints and disputes.
    
- Supports productivity and efficiency.
    
- Protects employee interests.
    
- Encourages teamwork and cooperation.
    

### [[Parties Involved in Industrial Relations]]

**1. Employers:** Provide employment, wages, facilities, and workplace direction.

**2. Employees:** Perform work and contribute to organizational goals.

**3. Trade Unions:** Represent workers and protect their interests.

**4. Government:** Establishes labour laws, regulates employment conditions, and may help resolve disputes.

### [[Functions of Industrial Relations]]


- Negotiating wages and working conditions.
    
- Handling employee grievances.
    
- Resolving workplace conflicts.
    
- Promoting employee welfare.
    
- Maintaining discipline.
    
- Encouraging cooperation.
    
- Supporting collective bargaining.

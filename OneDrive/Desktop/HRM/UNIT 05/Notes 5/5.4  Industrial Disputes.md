**Meaning:** An industrial dispute is a disagreement between employers and workers, or between groups of workers, concerning employment, working conditions, wages, or related matters.

### [[Causes of Industrial Disputes]]

1. Low wages and salary disagreements.
    
2. Poor working conditions.
    
3. Long working hours.
    
4. Unfair treatment of employees.
    
5. Job insecurity.
    
6. Disagreement over promotions.
    
7. Disciplinary actions.
    
8. Poor communication.
    
9. Lack of recognition.
    
10. Disagreements over company policies.
    

### [[Types of Industrial Disputes]]

**1. Interest Disputes:** Disagreements over new employment conditions, wages, or benefits.

**2. Rights Disputes:** Disagreements about the interpretation or application of existing agreements, rules, or legal rights.

**3. Individual Disputes:** Conflicts involving an individual employee, such as a grievance about disciplinary action.

**4. Collective Disputes:** Conflicts involving a group of employees or a trade union.

### Effects of Industrial Disputes

- Loss of production.
    
- Financial losses.
    
- Poor employer-employee relationships.
    
- Reduced employee morale.
    
- Delays in delivering products or services.
    
- Damage to organizational reputation.
    
- Possible loss of wages for workers during work stoppages.
    

### Prevention of Industrial Disputes

- Maintain effective communication.
    
- Pay fair wages.
    
- Provide safe working conditions.
    
- Respect employee rights.
    
- Handle grievances promptly.
    
- Encourage collective bargaining.
    
- Treat employees fairly.
    
- Involve workers in relevant decisions.
